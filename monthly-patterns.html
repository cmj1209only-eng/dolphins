📊 Florida Dolphin Sightings by Month

Comparing monthly sightings across Florida’s four coastal zones (2021–2026)

🔍 What This Graph Shows—and the Surprising Science Behind It

Hover over any bar segment or click a coastal zone in the legend to isolate it! Combining all 2,206 verified sightings (2021–2026), sightings climb to a peak of 343 in April, stay lively into May–July peak calving season (when over 81% of Sarasota Bay calves are born), and then drop by more than half in August–October.

Long-term photo-ID tracking shows that Sarasota Bay’s resident dolphins never migrate away—they live in their home bay all 12 months of the year! Instead, the spring peak and late-summer dip reflect human observer weather: cool, sunny winter and spring days bring thousands of boaters and beachgoers out with cameras, while scorching late-summer heat, afternoon lightning storms, and hurricanes keep people off the water.
