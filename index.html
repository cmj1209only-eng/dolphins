Sarasota Bay & Florida Bottlenose Dolphin Presence (2021–2026)

An interactive scientific explorer built from 2,206 verified visual sightings (Jan 2021 – Nov 2026)

This research platform investigates common bottlenose dolphin (Tursiops truncatus) sightings and seasonal presence from January 2021 through December 2026, pairing 2,206 verified GBIF visual sightings across Florida’s coastline (207 inside the Sarasota Bay focal boundary and 680 in adjacent Tampa Bay & Charlotte Harbor) with 50+ years of demographic context from the Sarasota Dolphin Research Program (Wells et al., 2025).
