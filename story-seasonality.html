🌦️ Why Sightings Drop in Late Summer

Dolphins never leave Sarasota Bay—so why do sightings plunge every August–October?

🐬 The Big Surprise: Sightings peak in March and drop by over two-thirds in August–October—yet Sarasota Bay’s dolphins stay in the bay all 12 months of the year! What actually changes is human weather: late-summer heat, afternoon lightning storms, and hurricane season keep people off the water.

🔑 Two Reasons Behind the Curve

  • Spring Tourism + Calm Seas (Jan–Apr): Cool, sunny dry-season weather puts thousands of boaters and beachgoers along Sarasota Bay’s passes.
  • Summer Moms & Calves (May–Jul) vs. Storm Season (Aug–Oct): Even though 81% of calves are born in May–July (Wells et al., 2025), late-summer storms push people indoors while underwater hydrophones still hear dolphins whistling away!
