📈🍼 2021–2026 Trends, Hurricanes & Calving Season

Six years of coastal sighting trends, major Gulf hurricanes, and May–July baby-dolphin calving

🌀🍼 Resilience Year After Year: Sightings dip temporarily when major hurricanes strike (Ian in 2022, Idalia in 2023, Helene & Milton in 2024) and rebound every spring—while May–July marks Sarasota Bay’s peak calving window, when over 81% of dolphin babies are born (Wells et al., 2025)!

📈 1. Quarterly Sightings & Major Gulf Hurricanes (2021–2026)

  • More cameras, steady dolphin community: Suncoast sightings nearly doubled from 98 in 2021 to 179 in 2026 as more people logged wildlife on iNaturalist, while Sarasota Bay’s annual birth rate (7.1%) nearly equals its annual loss rate (7.2%), keeping the resident population steady!
