📈 2021–2026 Trends & Major Hurricanes

Six years of rising coastal sightings—and sharp dips during major Gulf storms

🌀 Storms vs. Survival: Notice how sightings dip right when major hurricanes hit (Ian in 2022, Idalia in 2023, Helene & Milton in 2024)—then bounce right back the next spring! Resident Sarasota Bay dolphins stay put through hurricanes while damaged piers and marinas temporarily slow human sightings.

📌 Why Did Overall Sightings Climb From 2021 to 2026?

  • More smartphone cameras, stable dolphin numbers: Suncoast sightings nearly doubled from 98 in 2021 to 179 in 2026 mainly because more people log wildlife on apps like iNaturalist.
  • Balanced population: Wells et al. (2025) found Sarasota Bay’s annual birth rate (7.1%) almost perfectly matches its annual loss rate (7.2%), keeping the community remarkably steady!
