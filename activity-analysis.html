Time-of-Day Activity Explorer (2021–2026)

Investigating 2,101 timestamped visual sightings across hours, seasons, and coastal zones

Out of 2,206 sightings in our 2021–2026 dataset, 2,101 records (95.2%) include an exact local clock timestamp (105 records reported only the date and are excluded below rather than assigned an artificial hour).

Critical methodological note on effort: Visual sightings require daylight and human observers on boats, piers, or beaches. Sharp drop-offs after sunset reflect visual observer effort, not dolphins sleeping or leaving Sarasota Bay—passive acoustic studies using the Sarasota Bay Listening Network show that dolphins vocalize and forage actively throughout the night.
