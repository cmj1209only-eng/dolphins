🌦️☀️ Seasons & Time of Day (2021–2026)

How Florida weather shapes both which months—and which hours of the day—dolphins get spotted

🐬 One Big Weather Story: Sarasota Bay’s dolphins live in the bay 24 hours a day, all 12 months of the year—yet sightings swing dramatically by both season and clock hour because human boaters and beachgoers follow cool sunshine and avoid darkness, summer heat, and lightning storms!

🌦️ 1. Across the Seasons: Why Sightings Drop in Late Summer

Sightings peak in March during Florida’s sunny dry season and drop by over two-thirds in August–October during storm and hurricane season—even though 81% of Sarasota Bay calves are born in May–July (Wells et al., 2025):

🌤️ 3. Where Seasons & Time of Day Meet: Morning vs. Afternoon

Notice how the two patterns connect: in Summer, boaters head out early (Morning: 45.7%) to beat afternoon heat and lightning storms, whereas in Winter, mild sunny Afternoons (51.9%) take the lead!
